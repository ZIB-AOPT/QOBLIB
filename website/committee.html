Steering Committee

QOBLIB is maintained and developed by a steering committee of researchers from academia and industry. The committee oversees benchmark design, reviews contributions, and guides the long-term direction of the library.

Committee Responsibilities
Benchmark Design

Curating and evolving the ten problem classes, their instance generators, and reference solutions to ensure diverse, fair, and meaningful challenges for both classical and quantum solvers.

Submission Review

Reviewing pull requests — every PR requires approval by at least two committee members — resolving disputes about attribution and correctness, and operating the automated checker pipeline that validates every contribution.

Community & Standards

Maintaining the contributing guidelines, responding to community questions, and evolving the submission format as the benchmarking landscape matures.

Scientific Governance

Overseeing the scientific integrity of the benchmark, coordinating with the broader optimization community, and publishing updates on methodology and results.
